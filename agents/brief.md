# Salon Queue — Brief

## Problem
On weekends, customers wait 1–2 hours in the salon without knowing their turn.
Some leave, others argue about who came first. The salon needs a fair, visible queue
that customers can follow from their phone.

## Users
- **Customer**: joins and tracks the queue from a phone.
- **Receptionist**: adds walk-ins, calls the next customer, handles no-shows.
- **Barber**: sees the current customer, starts and finishes services.
- **Owner**: manages services, barbers on duty, and opens or closes the queue.

## Must-have features
1. Join the queue from a phone: choose 1–3 services and a barber (or "Any").
2. Live status: position, estimated wait, "Start coming" alert, "It's your turn!" alert.
3. Reception dashboard: add walk-ins, call next, mark no-show, cancel.
4. Barber screen: current customer, Start / Done, On break toggle.
5. Waiting-area TV: who is with which barber + next 5 tokens.
6. Owner settings: services (time + price), barbers on duty, open/close online queue, daily stats.
7. English and Gujarati labels with a language toggle.
8. Live sync: phone, tablet, and TV update within 5 seconds.

## Don't build
Online payment, future-date appointments, SMS/WhatsApp notifications, loyalty
points, multiple branches, customer accounts or passwords.

## Look & feel
Premium salon feel: dark charcoal + gold accent, rounded cards, clean, mobile-first.
All labels in English and Gujarati.
